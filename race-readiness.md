# 🎒 Race Readiness & Transition Blueprint

A comprehensive gear checklist, transition bag organization guide, taper protocol, and tactical execution manual for target races.

---

## 🧳 Transition Bag Packing Guide (IRONMAN & 70.3)

### 1. 🌅 Morning Clothes / Dry Bag (Post-Race)
* [ ] Warm sweatshirt / comfortable dry hoodie & sweatpants
* [ ] Recovery slides or comfortable sneakers
* [ ] Phone, car keys, ID, and post-race recovery beverage (protein shake)
* [ ] Pre-race warm-up clothing (throwaway sweatpants/jacket for chilly starts)

### 2. 🏊 Swim Gear
* [ ] Full-sleeve neoprene wetsuit (inspect seams and zipper cord)
* [ ] 2x Swim goggles (1 mirrored for bright sun, 1 clear for overcast/early wave)
* [ ] Anti-chafe balm (Body Glide / TriSlide for neck, wrists, and ankles)
* [ ] Official race swim cap + silicone base cap for cold water insulation
* [ ] Timing chip strapped to **left ankle** (opposite of bike drivetrain)

### 3. 🚴 T1 / Bike Bag & Cockpit Setup
* [ ] Aero helmet or road helmet (must be clipped on before touching bike)
* [ ] Cycling sunglasses (photochromic or polarized lenses)
* [ ] Tri-suit / racing kit (wore under wetsuit)
* [ ] Cycling shoes (with rubber bands if flying mount, or placed in bag)
* [ ] Cycling socks (rolled down for rapid slide-on)
* [ ] Front BTA (Between-The-Arms) aero hydration bottle filled with carb mix
* [ ] Frame bottles with concentrated liquid nutrition ($80\text{--}95\text{g}$ carbs/hr)
* [ ] Bento box packed with emergency backup gels and chew blocks
* [ ] Saddlebag / flat kit: 2x CO2 cartridges + inflator head, 1x TPU/butyl tube, tire levers, tubeless plug kit
* [ ] Electronic shifting batteries (Di2 / AXS) charged to 100%
* [ ] Bike computer mounted with route and power/HR data screens configured
* [ ] Tire pressure set to optimal PSI on race morning

### 4. 🏃 T2 / Run Bag
* [ ] Carbon-plated race super-shoes (equipped with elastic lock-laces)
* [ ] Running socks (fresh dry pair)
* [ ] Race number belt with bib pre-attached
* [ ] Running cap or breathable visor
* [ ] Sunglasses (if changing from bike glasses)
* [ ] Gel flask / race fuel pouch (enough for entire run)
* [ ] Anti-blister foot powder / anti-chafe stick

### 5. 🎒 Special Needs Bags (IRONMAN California 140.6 Only)
* **Bike Special Needs (Mile 56):**
  * [ ] Duplicate bottle of concentrated favorite drink mix (frozen night before)
  * [ ] Savory food option (e.g., small pretzels or rice cakes to break sweet flavor fatigue)
  * [ ] Spare CO2 cartridge + spare tube
  * [ ] Chain lube wipe / chamois cream packet
* **Run Special Needs (Mile 13):**
  * [ ] Favorite caffeine gels / chew sleeves
  * [ ] Fresh pair of dry running socks
  * [ ] Long-sleeve technical layer (in case temperature drops after sunset)

## 📅 Race Week Taper & Preparation Protocol

```text
7 Days Out  ──> Final race-pace tune-up workout (short intervals at target pace)
5 Days Out  ──> Deep clean & wax/lube drivetrain; new tires installed and test-ridden
3 Days Out  ──> Begin high-carb intake (8–10g carbs/kg body weight); reduce fiber
2 Days Out  ──> "The Money Night" of sleep — prioritize 8–9 hours of deep rest
1 Day Out   ──> 20-min easy shakeout (swim/bike/run) + gear check-in at transition
Race Day    ──> Wake up 3.5h before gun; 800–1,000 kcal carb-rich breakfast 3h prior
```

## 🎯 Race Day Tactical Guide

### 1. IRONMAN 70.3 Oceanside
* **Harbor Swim (1.2 mi):** Smooth rolling start. Avoid sprint surges off the beach. Find feet with steady stroke cadence (~32–34 strokes/min).
* **Camp Pendleton Bike (56 mi):**
  * **Crucial Rule:** Enforce an absolute power ceiling on **San Mateo hill** ($\le 95\%$ FTP). Surging above 100% on early climbs burns irreplaceable fast-twitch muscle glycogen.
  * Hold strict aero position on all flats; stay steady into coastal headwinds.
* **The Strand Run (13.1 mi):**
  * Mile 1: Expect heavy quad sensation from the bike. Keep cadence at 178+ spm and settle into **7:15 – 7:20 / mi**.
  * Miles 2–11: Settle into 7:10 / mi cruise pace. Take 1 gel every 3 miles with water at every aid station.
  * Miles 12–13.1: Empty the tank heading into the pier finish.

---

### 2. IRONMAN California
* **River Swim (2.4 mi):** Point-to-point downriver current. Sight the center channel current line for maximum free speed. Save legs by kicking lightly.
* **Delta Bike (112 mi):**
  * **Zero Coasting Course:** Flat delta terrain means non-stop pedaling for 5+ hours. Target **68–72% FTP**.
  * **Postural Management:** Perform regular 5-second neck and scapular retractions every 30 minutes to prevent neck extensor failure.
  * **Fueling Discipline:** Consume **80–95g carbs/hour** continuously from Mile 10 to Mile 105.
* **Capital Mall Marathon (26.2 mi):**
  * **First 10 Miles:** The crowd energy in Sacramento will trick you into running 7:30/mi. **Force an 8:05 – 8:15 / mi pace ceiling.**
  * Walk 30 seconds through every aid station to cleanly ingest fluids and sodium.

---

### 3. Standalone Sub-3:00 Marathon
* **Pacing Discipline:** Run an even split of **1:29:30 / 1:29:30** or slight negative split.
* **The 30K Checkpoint:** The marathon truly begins at Mile 20. If you have faithfully consumed 65g carbs/hr and maintained a 180 spm cadence, you will pass dozens of fading runners over the final 10 km.
