# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: IRONMAN 70.3 Oceanside Block

A periodized weekly schedule tailored for **IRONMAN 70.3 Oceanside (April 3, 2027)**. It balances **open-water swim endurance**, **punchy rolling-hill bike power (Camp Pendleton)**, **half marathon run economy off the bike**, and **stacked calisthenics** to maintain posture and power transfer without accumulated recovery fatigue.

---

## 🎯 Block Overview & Objectives

* **Target Event:** IRONMAN 70.3 Oceanside, California
* **Race Date:** Saturday, April 3, 2027
* **Disciplines & Distances:**
  * 🏊 **Swim:** 1.2 miles (1.9 km) — Oceanside Harbor (cool ocean water ~58–62°F, wetsuit legal, calm harbor navigation).
  * 🚴 **Bike:** 56 miles (90 km) — Camp Pendleton coastal and inland rolling hills (~2,800 ft / 850m elevation gain, including punchy climbs like San Mateo hill up to 8–10% grade).
  * 🏃 **Run:** 13.1 miles (21.1 km) — Two-loop coastal run along the Strand and Pacific pier ramps; flat with short punchy transition ramps.
* **Target Pacing Standard:** Sub-4:40 (Swim ~32:00, T1 ~3:30, Bike ~2:30:00 [22.4 mph / 80–83% FTP], T2 ~2:00, Run ~1:32:00 [7:01/mi pace]).

## 🏊🚴🏃 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Structural Recovery**
* **Cardio:** Complete Rest.
* **Mobility Protocol (20 mins):**
  1. **Shoulder Dislocates & Band Pull-Aparts:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps *(swim shoulder preservation)*.
  2. **Couch Stretch:** 2 minutes per side *(counteract bike hip flexion)*.
  3. **Thoracic Extension on Foam Roller:** 3 minutes continuous rolling and breathing.
  4. **Dead Hangs from Bar:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds *(spinal decompression)*.

---

### **Tuesday: CSS Swim Intervals + Lactate Threshold Run + Leg Strength**
* **Morning Swim: Critical Swim Speed (CSS) Threshold *(2,500m Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 400m easy swim + 4 $\times$ 50m dynamic drills (catch-up, fingertip drag) with 15s rest.
  * **Pre-Set:** $4 \times 100\text{m}$ progressive build 1–4 on 1:45 turnaround.
  * **Main Set:** $5 \times 200\text{m}$ @ **CSS Pace (Goal: 1:32 – 1:36 / 100m)** with 20 seconds rest between intervals.
  * **Cool-down:** 200m easy pull buoy / backstroke.
* **Afternoon Run: Lactate Threshold Intervals *(1:00 Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 15 mins easy Zone 2 + 4 $\times$ 50m strides.
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 2\text{ miles}$ @ **6:25 – 6:35 / mi pace** (Zone 4) with 2-minute easy jog recoveries.
  * **Cool-down:** 10 mins easy Zone 2 jog.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(knee stability for Pendleton descents)*.
  2. **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps/leg *(hamstring control)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Glute Bridges:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg *(glute activation)*.

---

### **Wednesday: Sweet Spot & Surge Cycling + Upper Body A**
* **Primary Cardio: Camp Pendleton Rolling Hill Simulation *(1:15 – 1:30 Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 15 mins easy Zone 2 with 3 $\times$ 30s high-cadence spin-ups (105+ RPM).
  * **Main Set (Sweet Spot with Torque Surges):**
    * 3 blocks of:
      * 10 mins @ **Sweet Spot (88–92% FTP / 80–85 RPM in aero position)**.
      * Directly into 2 mins @ **Threshold / Low Cadence Climb (98–105% FTP / 65–70 RPM out of saddle)** simulating San Mateo hill.
      * 4 mins easy recovery spin in Zone 1.
  * **Cool-down:** 10 mins easy high-cadence spin.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Ride):**
  1. **Strict Pull-ups / Chin-ups:** 4 sets $\times 8\text{--}12$ reps *(primary lat pull transfer for swimming catch)*.
  2. **Parallel Bar Dips or Decline Push-ups:** 4 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(tricep & chest push)*.
  3. **Inverted Ring Rows:** 3 sets $\times 12$ reps *(scapular retraction)*.
  4. **Hanging Knee Tucks / Leg Raises:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(anti-extension core)*.

---

### **Thursday: Aerobic Swim + Easy Flush Run + Posterior/Ankle Care**
* **Morning Swim: Aerobic Engine & Paddle Strength *(2,200m – 2,500m Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 300m choice easy.
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 600\text{m}$ steady Zone 2 aerobic pacing with pull buoy & medium paddles (Focus on high elbow catch and long stroke glide; 30s rest).
  * **Cool-down:** 200m easy backstroke / breaststroke.
* **Afternoon Run: Easy Aerobic Flush *(0:45 Total / 5 Miles)***
  * **Main Set:** Continuous easy running strictly in low Zone 2 ($< 75\%$ HRmax / conversational pace).
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Nordic Hamstring Curls (or slow glute bridges):** 3 sets $\times 6\text{--}8$ reps.
  2. **Tibialis Wall Raises:** 3 sets $\times 20$ reps *(shin splint prevention)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Eccentric Calf Raises:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg (4-second lowering phase).

---

### **Friday: Active Recovery Spin**
* **Primary Cardio: Flush Spin *(0:45 Total)***
  * **Main Set:** Flat terrain or indoor trainer, Zone 1 ($< 55\%$ FTP). Keep cadence silky smooth at 90–95 RPM. Flush accumulated lactate and prepare neuromuscular system for Saturday's brick.
* **Calisthenics:** None. Rest, hydrate with electrolytes, prepare Saturday ride fuel.

---

### **Saturday: Race Simulation Long Ride + Brick Run**
* **Primary Cardio 1: Rolling Aero Long Ride *(2:45 – 3:30 Total / 50–58 Miles)***
  * **Target Power:** Zone 2 to low Zone 3 ($75\text{--}82\%$ FTP average).
  * **Terrain:** Rolling courses with climbs mimicking Camp Pendleton.
  * **Aero Discipline:** Stay in aero tuck on all flats and false flats; practice drinking and eating without coming out of aero bars.
  * **Fueling:** Consume **70–85g carbs/hr** (gels, maltodextrin drink mix) and **600–750 mL fluid/hr**.
* **Primary Cardio 2: Immediate Transition Brick Run *(0:20 Total / 2.5–3.2 Miles)***
  * **Execution:** Rack bike, switch shoes within 60 seconds (simulating T2).
  * **Mile 1:** Settle into cadence (175–180 spm), overcome initial "dead leg" sensation; don't surge.
  * **Miles 2–3:** Hold target **70.3 Race Pace (6:50 – 7:00 / mi)** with tall posture and relaxed shoulders.

---

### **Sunday: Quality Long Run + Upper Body B**
* **Primary Cardio: Quality Half Marathon Long Run *(1:30 – 1:45 Total / 11–13 Miles)***
  * **Miles 1–8:** Steady Zone 2 aerobic running (7:40 – 8:15 / mi).
  * **Miles 9–12:** Progressive finish down to target race pace (**6:50 / mi**).
  * **Cool-down:** 0.5–1 mile easy jog.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Post-Run or PM):**
  1. **Ring Push-ups / Archer Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps.
  2. **Strict Pull-ups:** 3 sets $\times$ clean reps (leave 2 in reserve).
  3. **Pike Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 8\text{--}10$ reps *(deltoid & overhead stability)*.
  4. **Ab Wheel Rollouts or Hollow Body Hold:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds.

## 🎯 Oceanside 70.3 Race Execution Checklist

* **Swim Strategy:** Draft behind similarly paced swimmers in the harbor; save legs by using a 2-beat flutter kick until the final 200m.
* **T1 Execution:** Wetsuit strippers, helmet on before touching bike, quick mount line running.
* **Bike Pacing Rule:** **Cap power on climbs at 95% FTP.** Surging $> 110\%$ on San Mateo hill will destroy run legs at mile 8 of the run.
* **Nutrition Target:** Minimum 200g carbohydrates and 1,800mg sodium consumed on the bike before T2.
