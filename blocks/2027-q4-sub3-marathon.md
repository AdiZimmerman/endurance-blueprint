# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: Sub-3:00 Marathon Block

A specialized peak marathon training block designed to break the **3:00:00 marathon barrier (Target: 2:59:00 / 6:49.6 per mile)** in late 2027 (e.g., California International Marathon in December or a late-fall PR course). 

This schedule directly harnesses the monster aerobic engine, mitochondrial density, and fat-burning efficiency forged during the 2027 IRONMAN campaign, converting triathlete diesel endurance into sharp, relentless marathon velocity while maintaining hybrid cycling cross-training to protect connective tissue.

---

## 🎯 Block Overview & Objectives

* **Target Event:** Late 2027 PR Marathon (e.g., California International Marathon - CIM, Sacramento)
* **Goal Time:** **Sub-3:00:00** (Target execution: 2:59:00 | Average Pace: 6:49.6 / mi / 4:14.6 / km)
* **Primary Cardio Modality:** Pure marathon-specific road running (~50–65 miles / week)
* **Cross-Training Modality:** Low-impact Zone 1/2 road cycling (1–2 sessions/week) to accumulate aerobic volume with zero eccentric leg impact.
* **Calisthenics Priority:** Posterior chain durability (hamstring eccentrics), Achilles/tibialis stiffness, and anti-extension midline core to eliminate late-race posture collapse at Mile 20+.

## 🏃 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Mobility**
* **Cardio:** Complete Rest.
* **Structural Recovery Protocol (20 mins):**
  1. **Couch Stretch (Hip Flexor / Rectus Femoris):** 2 minutes per side.
  2. **Pigeon Stretch or 90-90 Hip Switch:** 2 minutes per side.
  3. **Thoracic Foam Rolling:** 3 minutes continuous rolling.
  4. **Dead Hangs from Pull-Up Bar:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds *(spinal decompression)*.

---

### **Tuesday: Lactate Threshold Cruise Intervals + Leg Strength**
* **Primary Cardio: Sustained Lactate Threshold Run *(1:15 Total / 10–11 Miles)***
  * **Warm-up:** 2 miles easy Zone 2 + dynamic mobility drills + 4 $\times$ 50m strides.
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 2.5\text{ miles}$ (or $2 \times 4\text{ miles}$) @ **Lactate Threshold Pace (6:25 – 6:35 / mi)** with 2.5 minutes easy jog recovery between sets.
  * **Cool-down:** 1.5 miles easy Zone 2.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(unilateral quad & glute drive)*.
  2. **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps/leg *(hamstring balance & pelvic level)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Glute Bridges:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg *(hip extension power)*.

---

### **Wednesday: Aerobic Flush Spin + Upper Body A**
* **Primary Cardio: Cycling Active Flush *(1:00 – 1:15 Total)***
  * **Main Set:** Flat terrain or smart trainer in Zone 1/low Zone 2 ($< 65\%$ FTP). Maintain a light, smooth cadence (90–95 RPM) to circulate blood through tired legs without impact loading.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Ride):**
  1. **Strict Deadhang Pull-ups:** 4 sets $\times 8\text{--}12$ reps *(thoracic upright posture & lat strength)*.
  2. **Parallel Bar Dips or Decline Push-ups:** 4 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps.
  3. **Inverted Ring Rows:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(scapular retraction for upright running carriage)*.
  4. **Hanging Knee/Leg Raises:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(anterior core stability)*.

---

### **Thursday: Marathon Goal Pace (MGP) Specific Run + Durability**
* **Primary Cardio: Marathon Pace Specificity Run *(1:15 – 1:30 Total / 11–13 Miles)***
  * **Warm-up:** 2 miles easy Zone 2 (7:45 – 8:15 / mi).
  * **Main Set:** $7\text{ miles}$ continuous @ **Marathon Goal Pace (6:48 – 6:50 / mi)** on flat or gently undulating road. Practice exact race cadence (176–182 spm) and breathing rhythm.
  * **Cool-down:** 1.5 miles easy jog.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Nordic Hamstring Curls (or slow glute bridges):** 3 sets $\times 6\text{--}8$ reps *(builds eccentric strength to prevent late-race hamstring spasms)*.
  2. **Tibialis Wall Raises:** 3 sets $\times 20\text{--}25$ reps *(anterior lower leg shield against shin splints)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Eccentric Calf Raises:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg (4-second slow descent on a step).

---

### **Friday: Easy Recovery Shakeout or Full Rest**
* **Primary Cardio:** Easy Aerobic Shakeout *(0:40 – 0:45 Total / 4–5 Miles)*
  * **Pace:** Strictly low Zone 1/2 (8:15 – 8:45 / mi). Soft surface (grass or crushed gravel) preferred.
* **Calisthenics:** None. Hydrate with electrolytes and eat carb-dense meals for the Sunday long run.

---

### **Saturday: Aerobic Base Run + Strides + Core B**
* **Primary Cardio: Easy Aerobic Run with Turnover *(0:50 Total / 6 Miles)***
  * **Pace:** Conversational Zone 2 (7:40 – 8:15 / mi).
  * **Turnover Finish:** $6 \times 80\text{m}$ fast strides (focus on quick foot turnover, tall torso, high knees, relaxed jaw; 60s walk back rest).
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Post-Run):**
  1. **Archer Push-ups or Ring Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps.
  2. **Strict Pull-ups:** 3 sets $\times$ clean reps.
  3. **Ab Wheel Rollouts / Hollow Body Hold:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds.
  4. **Side Planks:** 3 sets $\times 45$ seconds/side *(hip abductor and lateral trunk strength)*.

---

### **Sunday: Quality Progressive Long Run**
* **Primary Cardio: Marathon Long Run *(2:00 – 2:30 Total / 18–22 Miles)***
  * *Alternating Week Structure:*
    * **Week A (Aerobic Volume):** 20–22 miles steady Zone 2 (7:30 – 8:00 / mi) practicing fueling every 4 miles.
    * **Week B (Fast Finish Specificity):** 18 miles total:
      * Miles 1–11: Easy Zone 2 (7:40 – 8:00 / mi).
      * Miles 12–17: **6 miles locked @ Marathon Goal Pace (6:45 – 6:50 / mi)** on tired legs.
      * Mile 18: 1 mile easy jog cool-down.
  * **Fueling Execution:** Take 1 carbohydrate gel (25–30g carbs) with water at miles 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19.

## 🎯 Sub-3 Execution Rules

1. **First 10K Discipline:** The most common failure mode is running the first 10K in 41:00 (6:35/mi) due to taper adrenaline. **Enforce an absolute speed cap of 6:48/mi for the first 5 miles.**
2. **Even / Negative Splits:** A half-marathon split of **1:29:30** leaves a 30-second cushion. Do not bank time early; bank energy.
3. **Mid-Race Fueling:** Consume **60–75g carbs/hour** (every 25–30 minutes) + water at every aid station. Glycogen depletion at mile 20 is purely a fueling miscalculation, not an aerobic failure.
4. **Cadence Check:** When leg fatigue arrives at mile 21, consciously increase arm swing cadence to keep foot turnover above 178 spm.
