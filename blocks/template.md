## 📈 Periodization & Deload Guidelines
* **Cycle Cadence:** 3 weeks progressive overload followed by 1 week consolidation / deload.
* **Deload Modifications:**
  * Reduce overall cardio volume by **30–40%**.
  * Maintain intensity frequency (keep strides or short threshold touches), but cut total interval volume in half.
  * Reduce calisthenics volume to 2 sets per movement to replenish nervous system reserves.
