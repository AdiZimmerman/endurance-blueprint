# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: Sub-1:30 Half Marathon Block

This weekly schedule integrates **sub-threshold and V̇O₂ max running intervals**, **long aerobic cycling**, and **stacked strength work** to build speed and structural resilience. Tuesday and Thursday are the two quality run sessions; Sunday is a long aerobic/race-specific run supported by Saturday's bike volume.

---

<a id="pre-run-activation"></a>
## 🔥 Dynamic Mobility & Pre-Run Activation *(7–10 min — before every run)*

> Run before lacing up on **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday.** Goal: turn on stabilizers, open joints, prime the neuromuscular system.

| # | Exercise | Reps / Duration | Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Ankle Mobilization** | 10 reps/side | Kneel on one knee; drive front knee past toes keeping heel flat |
| 2 | **90/90 Hip Transitions** | 8–10 reps/side | Move smoothly between internal and external rotation; stay tall and do not force end range |
| 3 | **Dynamic Cossack Squat / Lateral Lunge** | 5–6 reps/side | Sit into one hip while the other leg stays long; control the knee and keep the foot grounded |
| 4 | **Dynamic Runner's Lunge + Twist** | 5 reps/side | Step into a controlled lunge, rotate toward the front leg, then return immediately; no prolonged hold |
| 5 | **Great Toe Extensions** | 8–10 reps/side | Gently extend the big toe under light bodyweight, then release; no aggressive loaded stretch |
| 6 | **Single-Leg Glute Bridge** | 10 reps/side | Bridge on one leg; squeeze glute for 2 sec at the top |
| 7 | **Banded Monster Walks** | 15 steps fwd + 15 back | Mini-band above knees; diagonal steps; wakes up glute med |
| 8 | **Knee Hug to Hip Opener** | 5 reps/side | Pull knee to chest, then open it out to the side; mobilizes hip capsule |

## 📊 Weekly Overview

| Day | Primary Workout | Strength | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Rest | — | Full rest, foam rolling, mobility. |
| **Tuesday** | **Lactate Threshold Run** *(1:00)* | **Lower-Body** *(4 exercises)* | Hard day 1: threshold speed + glute/hamstring/calf loading. |
| **Wednesday** | **Easy Recovery Run** *(1:00 @ Zone 2)* | — | True recovery flush between hard sessions. |
| **Thursday** | **Track / V̇O₂ Max Run** *(1:00)* | **Core & Hip Stability** *(3 exercises)* | Hard day 2: aerobic ceiling plus trunk, adductor, and lateral-hip control. |
| **Friday** | **Easy Flush Ride** *(1:00–1:15)* | — | Pre-weekend recovery spin. |
| **Saturday** | **Long Road Cycling** *(2:30–3:30 @ Zone 2)* | — | Massive aerobic base, zero leg impact. |
| **Sunday** | **Quality Long Run** *(1:45–2:15)* | — | HM race specificity: progressive long run with race-pace finish. |

> **Structure:** Hard → Easy → Hard → Easy → weekend volume block. Tuesday and Thursday are separated by an easy run, while the weekend is intentionally a lower-impact volume block with one quality long run.

## 🏃 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Active Recovery**
* **Cardio:** Complete rest.
* **Recovery:** 15–20 min foam rolling — quads, IT bands, calves, thoracic spine.

---

### **Tuesday: Threshold Run + Lower-Body Strength**

**Order:** Activation → [Optional Plyos] → Threshold Run → Strength

* **Pre-Run:** [Dynamic Mobility & Pre-Run Activation](#pre-run-activation)

* **[Optional] Advanced Plyometrics** *(before the run, when fresh — see [Strength Library → Advanced Plyometrics](../strength_library.md#advanced-plyometrics))*
  * Depth Jumps · Alternate Leg Bounding · Single-Leg Line Hops · Broad Jumps
  * Use **one** plyometric session per week at most; Tuesday is the primary slot. Skip it during high-fatigue weeks or if landing quality drops.

* **Threshold Run** *(1:00 total)*
  * **Warm-up:** 15 min easy Zone 2 + dynamic drills.
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 2\text{ mi}$ @ **6:25–6:35 / mi** (Zone 4) with 2:30–3:00 min easy jog recovery.
  * **Cool-down:** 10 min easy Zone 2.

* **Mandatory Strength** *(immediately post-run)*
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squat** — 3 × 10–12 reps/leg *(primary quad & glute driver; control depth & knee tracking)*
  2. **Nordic Curl** — 3 × 5–8 reps *(slow eccentric descent; highest-evidence hamstring injury prevention)*
  3. **Single-Leg Calf Raise** — 3 × 12–15 reps/leg, off a step *(3-sec eccentric; builds Achilles tendon stiffness)*
  4. **Tibialis Raise** — 3 × 15–20 reps *(back against wall; pause 1 sec at top; anterior shin durability)*

  > **Supplementary options:** Step-Ups, Single-Leg RDL, Single-Leg Hip Thrust, Seated Soleus Raise, Pogo Hops, Lateral Band Walks — see [Strength Library → Lower-Body supplementary options](../strength_library.md#lower-body-supplementary-options).

---

### **Wednesday: Easy Recovery Run**

* **Pre-Run:** [Dynamic Mobility & Pre-Run Activation](#pre-run-activation)
* **Cardio:** Easy Zone 2 Run *(0:50–1:00 total)*
  * HR capped in low Z2. Fully conversational. No watch-pacing — run by feel.
* **Strength:** None. This day exists to recover from Tuesday and prime Thursday.

> The easy day between hard sessions is not wasted mileage — it's what makes Thursday's VO₂ session high quality.

---

### **Thursday: V̇O₂ Max Track Run + Core & Hip Stability**

* **Pre-Run:** [Dynamic Mobility & Pre-Run Activation](#pre-run-activation)

* **V̇O₂ Max Run** *(1:00 total)*
  * **Warm-up:** 15 min easy Zone 2 + 4 × 50m strides.
  * **Main Set:** @ **6:00–6:15 / mi** (Zone 5) with 90-sec easy jog recoveries.
    * *Weeks 1–2:* $6 \times 800\text{m}$
    * *Weeks 3–6:* $5 \times 1{,}000\text{m}$
    * *Weeks 7–10:* $4 \times 1{,}200\text{m}$
  * **Cool-down:** 10 min easy Zone 2.

* **Mandatory Strength** *(immediately post-run)*
  1. **Copenhagen Plank** — 3 × 20–30 sec/side *(top foot on bench, bottom leg hanging; adductor & pelvic load)*
  2. **Side Plank + Top-Leg Lift** — 3 × 10–12 reps/side *(fires glute med; controls knee collapse mid-stride)*
  3. **Hollow Body Hold or Ab Wheel** — 3 × 45–60 sec / 3 × 12–15 reps *(pelvic control; eliminates lower-back hyperextension)*

  > **Supplementary options:** Dead Bug, Pallof Press, Loaded Hip-Flexor March — see [Strength Library → Core supplementary options](../strength_library.md#core-supplementary-options).

---

### **Friday: Active Recovery Spin**
* **Cardio:** Flush Ride *(1:00–1:15 total)*
  * Zone 1/2. High cadence (85+ RPM). Metabolic waste clearance before the weekend block.
* **Strength:** None.

---

### **Saturday: Long Aerobic Road Bike**
* **Cardio:** Long Road Ride *(2:30–3:30 total)*
  * Continuous Zone 2. Steady pacing. **60–80g carbs/hr**.
* **Strength:** None.

---

### **Sunday: Quality Long Run**
* **Pre-Run:** [Dynamic Mobility & Pre-Run Activation](#pre-run-activation)
* **Cardio:** Progressive Long Run *(1:45–2:15 / 12–14 miles)*
  * **Week A — Race-Pace Finish:** Miles 1–9 easy Zone 2 → Miles 10–13 at **6:50 / mi** → 1 mile cool-down.
  * **Week B — Aerobic Base:** 12–14 miles steady Zone 2.

  > Keep Sunday focused on the long run. Do not add advanced plyometrics before or after this session; the Tuesday slot is sufficient.

---

## ⚡ Optional Upgrades

*Non-mandatory. Add only with extra bandwidth and full recovery from primary sessions.*

* **Wednesday Evening — Flush Spin (+45 min):** Light Zone 1/2 spin 6–8 hrs after a hard week's track work.
* **Saturday Post-Bike — Brick Run (+20 min):** 15–20 min easy Zone 2 jog immediately off the bike.
* **Monday / Friday Evening — Tissue Work (+15 min):** Hip, ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic spine mobility + foam rolling.
