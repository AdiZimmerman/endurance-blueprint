# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: Sub-1:30 Half Marathon Block

This weekly schedule is built for high-performance endurance development and bodyweight strength. It integrates **sub-threshold and V̇O₂ max running intervals**, **long aerobic cycling**, and **stacked calisthenics** to maximize speed and maintain an athletic frame without excessive recovery debt or complex split-day logistics.

---

## 🔥 Pre-Run Activation Protocol *(5 min — before every run)*

> Run this sequence before lacing up on **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday**. Goal: turn on stabilizers, open joints, and prime the neuromuscular system before any intensity.

| # | Exercise | Reps / Duration | Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Ankle Mobilization** | 10 reps/side | Kneel on one knee; drive front knee forward past toes keeping heel flat on the floor |
| 2 | **Runner's Lunge + Twist** | 5 reps/side | Deep forward lunge; rotate torso toward ceiling, reaching arm up; opens hip flexors & thoracic spine |
| 3 | **Single-Leg Glute Bridge** | 10 reps/side | Bridge up on one leg, squeeze glute for 2 seconds at the top; activates posterior chain pre-run |
| 4 | **Banded Monster Walks** | 15 steps forward + 15 steps back | Mini-band above knees; diagonal forward/backward steps; wakes up glute med & hip abductors |
| 5 | **Knee Hug to Hip Opener** | 5 reps/side | Pull knee to chest while standing, then open it out to the side; mobilizes hip capsule & piriformis |

## 🏃 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Active Recovery**
* **Cardio:** Complete Rest.
* **Recovery Work:** 15–20 minutes of targeted foam rolling (quads, IT bands, calves, thoracic spine) and dynamic mobility.

---

### **Tuesday: Lactate Threshold Run + Lower-Body Strength & Plyometrics**
* **Pre-Run:** [5-min Activation Protocol](#-pre-run-activation-protocol-5-min--before-every-run) *(ankle mob, lunge + twist, SL glute bridge, monster walks, knee hug)*
* **Primary Cardio:** Lactate Threshold Run *(1:00 Total)*
  * **Warm-up:** 15 minutes easy Zone 2 running + dynamic drills.
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 2\text{ miles}$ @ **6:25 – 6:35 / mi pace** (Zone 4) with **2:30 – 3:00 minute** easy jog recoveries between sets.
  * **Cool-down:** 10 minutes easy Zone 2.

* **[Optional] Advanced Plyometrics** *(do before strength work if adding — see schedule note below)*
  * Perform on **1–2 days/week** when legs feel fresh. Tuesday is the primary day; Sunday long run day is the secondary option. Skip if legs are fatigued or during deload weeks.
  1. **Depth Jumps:** 4 sets $\times 5$ reps — step off a 12–18" box/stair, land on forefeet and immediately explode straight up. Minimal ground contact.
  2. **Alternate Leg Bounding:** 3 sets $\times$ 20m — exaggerated, high-powered running leaps over grass for maximum air time per stride.
  3. **Single-Leg Line Hops:** 3 sets $\times$ 15 reps/side — rapid side-to-side hops over a line on one foot with a stiff ankle. Maximizes reactive stiffness.
  4. **Broad Jumps:** 4 sets $\times$ 4 reps — jump forward off two feet for maximum distance; land softly and freeze for 2 seconds before resetting.

* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run / Post-Plyos) — Lower-Body Strength:**
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(focus on depth & knee alignment; primary quad & glute driver)*
  2. **Step-Ups:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(knee-height box or bench; drive through the heel; builds unilateral push-off power)*
  3. **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps/leg *(posterior chain & hip hinge balance; control the descent)*
  4. **Single-Leg Hip Thrust:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps/leg *(shoulders on bench; max glute extension at top)*
  5. **Pogo Hops:** 3 sets $\times 20\text{--}30$ reps *(ankle-stiff, minimal ground contact; trains elastic energy return in Achilles/calf spring — always do last)*

  > **Optional add-on:** **Lateral Band Walks** — 2 sets × 15 steps/side before or after as a glute-med activation primer.

---

### **Wednesday: Track V̇O₂ Max Run + Upper Body, Hip Stability & Core**
* **Pre-Run:** [5-min Activation Protocol](#-pre-run-activation-protocol-5-min--before-every-run) *(ankle mob, lunge + twist, SL glute bridge, monster walks, knee hug)*
* **Primary Cardio:** Track V̇O₂ Max Speed Run *(1:00 Total)*
  * **Warm-up:** 15 minutes easy Zone 2 running + 4 $\times$ 50m strides.
  * **Main Set:** V̇O₂ Max Intervals @ **6:00 – 6:15 / mi pace** (Zone 5) with 90-second easy jog recoveries.
    * *Weeks 1–2 Progression:* $6 \times 800\text{m}$
    * *Weeks 3–6 Progression:* $5 \times 1,000\text{m}$
    * *Weeks 7–10 Progression:* $4 \times 1,200\text{m}$
  * **Cool-down:** 10 minutes easy Zone 2.
* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run) — Upper Body, Hip Stability & Core:**
  1. **Pull-ups / Chin-ups:** 3–4 sets $\times$ max clean reps *(RPE 8–9, stop 1–2 reps before failure)*
  2. **Dips or Decline Push-ups:** 3–4 sets $\times 10\text{--}15$ reps
  3. **Inverted Rows / Ring Rows:** 3 sets $\times 12$ reps *(horizontal pulling for posture)*
  4. **Copenhagen Plank:** 3 sets $\times 20\text{--}30$ sec/side *(top foot on bench, bottom leg hanging; adductor & hip-pelvic load; key groin/pelvis resilience for runners)*
  5. **Side Plank + Top-Leg Lift:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/side *(hold side plank, lift top leg to hip height; fires glute med & lateral hip stabilizers that control knee collapse mid-stride)*
  6. **Hanging Leg Raises / Knee Tucks:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(anterior core, hip flexor strength)*

  > **Optional add-on:** **Dead Bug or Pallof Press** — 2–3 sets × 8–10 reps as a low-load anti-rotation core finisher. Best on easier effort days when you have a few extra minutes.

---

### **Thursday: Easy Recovery Run + Lower Leg, Posterior & Hip-Flexor Care**
* **Pre-Run:** [5-min Activation Protocol](#-pre-run-activation-protocol-5-min--before-every-run) *(ankle mob, lunge + twist, SL glute bridge, monster walks, knee hug)*
* **Primary Cardio:** Easy Recovery Run *(0:50 – 1:00 Total)*
  * **Main Set:** Continuous Zone 2 running (Heart Rate capped in low Z2 / conversational pace).
* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run) — Lower Leg & Posterior Protocol:**
  1. **Nordic Curl:** 3 sets $\times 5\text{--}8$ reps *(feet anchored, slow eccentric descent; gold standard eccentric hamstring strength)*
  2. **Tibialis Raise:** 3 sets $\times 15\text{--}20$ reps *(back flat against wall; pause 1s at top; progress by moving feet farther from wall)*
  3. **Seated Soleus Raise:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps *(seated, knees ~90°, weighted plate on thighs; knee bent isolates soleus which absorbs 6–8× BW per stride)*
  4. **Single-Leg Calf Raise:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps/leg *(straight leg, off a step; 3-second eccentric descent; targets gastrocnemius & Achilles tendon stiffness)*
  5. **Tibialis Anterior Heel Walks:** 3 sets $\times$ 30–45 sec *(toes lifted off ground; direct anterior shin endurance loading)*
  6. **Loaded Hip-Flexor March:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(standing, drive knee up to hip height against a band or weight; strengthens iliopsoas for stride length & running-specific hip flexor durability)*
  7. **Banded Ankle Inversion:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/side *(band anchored low; pull foot up & inward; posterior tibialis & arch control)*

  > **Execution tip:** Use a 3-second eccentric tempo on all raise variations. See [benchmarks.md → Lower Leg Strength & Shin Health](../benchmarks.md) for full exercise detail, loading progressions, and biomechanical cues.

  > **Optional add-on:** **Lateral Band Walks** — 2 sets × 15 steps/side if not done Tuesday; pairs well here as a low-intensity glute-med warmup.

---

### **Friday: Active Recovery Spin**
* **Primary Cardio:** Flush Ride *(1:00 – 1:15 Total)*
  * **Main Set:** Easy cycling in Zone 1/2. Keep cadence light, smooth, and high (85+ RPM) to flush metabolic waste from mid-week running workouts.
* **Calisthenics:** None.

---

### **Saturday: Long Aerobic Road Bike**
* **Primary Cardio:** Long Road Bike Ride *(2:30 – 3:30 Total)*
  * **Main Set:** Continuous Zone 2 aerobic ride. Focus on steady pacing, staying relaxed on hills, and consuming **60–80g of carbohydrates per hour**.
* **Calisthenics:** None.

---

### **Sunday: Quality Long Run + Upper Body B**
* **Pre-Run:** [5-min Activation Protocol](#-pre-run-activation-protocol-5-min--before-every-run) *(ankle mob, lunge + twist, SL glute bridge, monster walks, knee hug)*
* **Primary Cardio:** Quality Progressive Long Run *(1:45 – 2:15 / 12–14 Miles)*
  * **Alternate Weekly Formats:**
    * **Week A (Race-Pace Finish):** Miles 1–9 easy Zone 2 $\rightarrow$ Miles 10–13 fast finish at target half marathon pace (**6:50 / mi pace**) $\rightarrow$ 1 mile cool-down.
    * **Week B (Aerobic Base):** 12–14 miles continuous steady Zone 2.
* **Calisthenics (Post-Run or Evening):**
  1. **Archer Push-ups or Ring Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 8\text{--}12$ reps
  2. **Pull-ups:** 3 sets $\times$ max clean reps
  3. **Pike Push-ups (Shoulder Focus):** 3 sets $\times 8\text{--}10$ reps
  4. **Ab Wheel Rollouts or Hollow Body Hold:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds

  > **[Optional] Advanced Plyometrics on Sunday:** If Tuesday's plyo session was the first of the week and you're in a higher-volume week (weeks 3–8), you may add a second plyo session here immediately *before* the long run warm-up — but only if resting HR is normal and legs feel springy. Alternate Leg Bounding and Single-Leg Line Hops are the best choices; skip Depth Jumps before a 14-mile run.

## ⚡ Optional Upgrades (If Time & Energy Permit)

*These sessions are 100% non-mandatory. Only add them if you have extra bandwidth and feel fully recovered from primary workouts.*

* **Wednesday Evening — Active Flush Spin (+45 mins):**
  * *Protocol:* 45-minute very light Zone 1/2 spin on the trainer or road 6–8 hours after morning track work.
  * *Benefit:* Accelerates lactate clearance and adds low-stress aerobic volume.
* **Thursday Morning — Extended Zone 2 Base (+30 mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Extend Thursday's easy recovery run from 60 to **80–90 minutes**.
  * *Benefit:* Drives deeper mitochondrial density and fat-oxidation adaptations.
* **Saturday Post-Bike — Brick Transition Run (+20 mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Immediately after stepping off the 3-hour Saturday ride, do a 15–20 minute easy Zone 2 transition jog.
  * *Benefit:* Trains neuromuscular adaptation to running on pre-fatigued legs with minimal joint wear.
* **Monday / Friday Evening — Mobility & Tissue Recovery (+15 mins):**
  * *Protocol:* 15 minutes of targeted mobility work (hips, ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic spine) and foam rolling.
  * *Benefit:* Decompresses joints and preserves smooth running mechanics.
