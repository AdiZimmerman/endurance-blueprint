# Escape From Alcatraz Triathlon Block

**Event Date:** Sunday, June 6, 2027

## Overview

* **Location:** Alcatraz Island, San Francisco, CA
* **Disciplines & Distances:**
  * 🏊 **Swim:** 1.5 mi (2.4 km) – Open‑water around Alcatraz Island, cold water (~55 °F), wetsuit legal.
  * 🚴 **Bike:** 18 mi (29 km) – Bay Bridge crossing, rolling coastal hills, windy San Francisco Bay.
  * 🏃 **Run:** 8 mi (13 km) – Historic waterfront marathon route with short steep sections on the Presidio trail.

## Objectives

- Build fast open‑water swim endurance and sight‑gate efficiency.
- Develop power for short, hilly coastal bike legs and strong handling in windy conditions.
- Sharpen transition speed (T1/T2) and run pacing for an 8‑mile fast‑finish.
- Incorporate core and upper‑body work to maintain posture during bike and protect shoulders in swim.

## Detailed Daily Workouts

### Monday – Rest & Mobility
* **Mobility (20 min):** Dead hangs – 4 × 45‑60 s; Prone cobra/Y‑T‑W – 3 × 10; Couch stretch – 2 min/side; Foam‑roll quads, IT‑band & thoracic spine – 5 min.

### Tuesday – Swim + Tempo Run + Leg Strength
**Swim (2,000 m):** Warm‑up 300 m easy + 4 × 50 m drills; Main set 5 × 200 m @ CSS pace (1:30 / 100 m) with 20 s rest; Cool‑down 200 m.
**Run (0:30 @ Zone 4):** Warm‑up 10 min easy + 4 × 100 m strides; Main set 4 × 800 m @ 6:20 / mi pace, 2 min jog; Cool‑down 10 min.
**Leg Strength (post‑run, 20 min):** Bulgarian split squats – 3 × 10‑12/leg; Single‑leg RDL – 3 × 10; Single‑leg glute bridges – 3 × 15.

### Wednesday – Hill Bike + Upper Body A
**Bike (30 min):** Warm‑up 10 min Zone 2; 4 × 4‑min hill repeats @ 95‑100 % FTP, 3 min easy spin; Cool‑down 10 min.
**Upper Body A (post‑ride, 20 min):** Strict pull‑ups – 4 × 8‑12; Parallel bar dips – 4 × 12‑15; Inverted rows – 3 × 12; Hanging knee tucks – 3 × 12‑15.

### Thursday – Aerobic Swim + Easy Run + Posterior Chain
**Swim (1,800 m):** Warm‑up 200 m; 4 × 400 m @ Zone 2 (1:45 / 100 m) with 20 s rest; Cool‑down 200 m.
**Run (0:30 @ Zone 2):** Easy 3‑4 mi on soft surface.
**Posterior Chain (15 min):** Nordic hamstring curls – 3 × 6‑8; Tibialis wall raises – 3 × 20; Single‑leg eccentric calf raises – 3 × 15 per leg.

### Friday – Recovery Spin
**Spin (0:45 @ Zone 1):** Low resistance, 90‑95 RPM, no calisthenics. Post‑session: 30‑40 g carbs, hydration.

### Saturday – Long Bike + Brick Run
**Bike (1.5 h, 18 mi):** Target power 70‑80 % FTP, cadence 85‑95 RPM; practice wind handling; fuel 60‑70 g carbs / hr.
**Brick Run (0:20 @ 5K‑pace):** Quick bike‑to‑run transition (< 45 s); run 3 mi at 6:30‑6:45 / mi.

### Sunday – Long Run + Core & Upper Body B
**Run (1 h, 8 mi):** 5 mi steady Zone 2 (8:30‑9:00 / mi) then 3 mi progressive finish at race pace (6:45‑7:00 / mi). Cool‑down 0.5 mi.
**Core & Upper Body B (post‑run, 20 min):** Ring push‑ups – 3 × 10‑12; Strict pull‑ups – 3 × max (2‑rep reserve); Pike push‑ups – 3 × 8‑10; Ab wheel rollouts – 3 × 45‑60 s.

## Race‑Day Execution Checklist – Escape From Alcatraz
* **Swim:** Practice sight‑gates and drafting; wear wetsuit for cold water.
* **T1:** Quick removal of wetsuit, helmet on, shoes ready; aim < 30 s.
* **Bike:** Keep cadence high (90‑95 RPM), stay aero, manage wind; target 70‑80 % FTP on climbs.
* **T2:** Shoes within 5 ft of bike; practice < 20 s change.
* **Run:** Maintain upright posture on waterfront climbs; aim for 6:30‑6:45 / mi finish.
* **Nutrition:** 60‑70 g carbs / hr on bike, 30‑40 g carbs / hr on run; electrolytes every 30‑45 min.
