# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: Danube Cycle Path (Donauradweg) Block

This weekly schedule prepares the endurance engine, saddle resilience, and postural durability for multi-day bikepacking and touring along the Danube Cycle Path (Donauradweg). It emphasizes **back-to-back high-volume Zone 2 cycling**, **loaded gear simulation**, **posture-protecting calisthenics**, and **maintenance running** to keep legs versatile and impact-adapted.

## 🚴 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Postural Recovery**
* **Cardio:** Complete Rest.
* **Mobility Protocol (20 mins):**
  1. **Couch Stretch (Hip Flexor / Quad):** 2 minutes per side.
  2. **Thoracic Foam Rolling & Cat-Cow:** 3 minutes continuous flow.
  3. **Pigeon Stretch / 90-90 Hip Switch:** 2 minutes per side.
  4. **Dead Hang from Pull-up Bar:** 3 sets $\times$ 45–60 seconds (spinal decompression).

---

### **Tuesday: Sweet Spot / Cadence Intervals + Scapular Posture**
* **Primary Cardio:** Sweet Spot Cycling Workout *(1:15 – 1:30 Total)*
  * **Warm-up:** 15 minutes progressive Zone 1 to Zone 2 with 3 $\times$ 30s high-cadence spin-ups (105+ RPM).
  * **Main Set:** $3 \times 15\text{ mins}$ @ **Sweet Spot (88–94% FTP / Zone 4a)** with 5-minute easy Zone 1 spinning between sets. Focus on steady, relaxed upper body and cadence at 85–90 RPM.
  * **Cool-down:** 10–15 minutes easy spin in Zone 1.
* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Ride):**
  1. **Inverted Ring Rows / Horizontal Rows:** 4 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(focus on full scapular retraction)*
  2. **Face Pulls (with band or rings):** 3 sets $\times 15\text{--}20$ reps *(rear deltoids & rotator cuff)*
  3. **Prone Y-T-W Raises:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps each position *(thoracic endurance for handlebars)*
  4. **Hanging Knee/Leg Raises:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(anterior core stability)*

---

### **Wednesday: Maintenance Aerobic Run + Hip & Knee Durability**
* **Primary Cardio:** Easy Aerobic Maintenance Run *(0:40 – 0:45 Total / 4–5 Miles)*
  * **Pace:** Easy Zone 2 conversational pace (7:45 – 8:30 / mi).
  * **Finish:** 4 $\times$ 80m strides at mile-pace turnover to maintain neuromuscular snap and ankle spring.
* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets $\times 12$ reps/leg *(unilateral quad & glute drive)*
  2. **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps/leg *(hamstring balance & pelvic level)*
  3. **Tibialis Wall Raises:** 3 sets $\times 20$ reps *(anterior shin protection)*
  4. **Single-Leg Calf Raises (Slow eccentric):** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg *(Achilles tendon durability)*

---

### **Thursday: Aerobic Tempo / Rolling Ride + Upper Body Circuit**
* **Primary Cardio:** Sustained Aerobic Tempo Ride *(1:30 – 2:00 Total)*
  * **Main Set:** Continuous riding in upper Zone 2 to low Zone 3 (70–82% FTP). Practice settling into the touring cadence (85–92 RPM) and staying relaxed on varied elevations.
* **Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Ride):**
  1. **Strict Pull-ups:** 4 sets $\times$ sub-maximal clean reps (leave 2 reps in reserve).
  2. **Parallel Bar Dips or Push-ups:** 3–4 sets $\times 15\text{--}20$ reps.
  3. **Pike Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 8\text{--}10$ reps *(overhead shoulder integrity).*
  4. **Plank with Shoulder Taps:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds *(rotational core control).*

---

### **Friday: Active Recovery Spin**
* **Primary Cardio:** Easy Flush Spin *(0:45 – 1:00 Total)*
  * **Main Set:** Low gear, flat terrain, Zone 1 ($< 55\%$ FTP or HR $< 65\%$). High cadence (90–95 RPM) to circulate blood flow through legs without accumulating training stress.
* **Calisthenics:** None. Focus on hydration and sleep.

---

### **Saturday: Loaded Long Ride 1 (Touring Simulation)**
* **Primary Cardio:** Loaded Long-Distance Bikepacking Simulation *(3:30 – 5:00 Total / 80–120 km)*
  * **Setup:** Ride with full or partial touring luggage (frame bag, saddle bag, panniers ~8–12 kg load).
  * **Pace:** Strict Zone 2 aerobic pacing (60–72% FTP). Practice constant pressure on pedals without surging on climbs.
  * **Fueling:** Consume **60–80g carbs/hr** and **500–750 mL fluid/hr** consistently from minute 30 onward.
* **Calisthenics:** None.

---

### **Sunday: Back-to-Back Endurance Ride 2 + Core Tune-up**
* **Primary Cardio:** Consecutive-Day Aerobic Ride *(2:30 – 3:30 Total / 60–85 km)*
  * **Setup:** Same loaded or semi-loaded bike setup.
  * **Pace:** Low-to-mid Zone 2. The goal is training the neuromuscular and metabolic pathways to perform smoothly on pre-fatigued muscles.
* **Calisthenics (Post-Ride):**
  1. **Ab Wheel Rollouts or Hollow Body Hold:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps or 45s hold.
  2. **Side Planks:** 3 sets $\times 45$ seconds/side.
  3. **Bird-Dog (Isometric Holds):** 3 sets $\times 30$ seconds/side *(lumbar stabilization).*

## 🎯 Tour & Execution Specifics: Danube Cycle Path

* **Route Profile:** Primarily flat to gently descending along the Danube river corridor (Passau $\to$ Linz $\to$ Vienna $\to$ Bratislava $\to$ Budapest) with occasional river bluff rollers and crosswinds.
* **Loaded Cruising Speed Target:** 20 – 25 km/h (12.5 – 15.5 mph) including regular photo and navigation pauses.
* **Target Daily Tour Mileage:** 75 – 110 km/day over 8–12 consecutive days.
* **Gear & Bike Testing Checklist:**
  - [ ] Saddle pressure mapping (zero numbness or hot spots after 4+ hours).
  - [ ] Cockpit ergonomics (handlebar tape thickness, inner bar ends / aerobars for hand position variety).
  - [ ] Tire selection: 35–42mm fast-rolling gravel/touring tires (e.g. Schwalbe Marathon / Continental Terra Speed) at optimal tire pressure.
  - [ ] On-bike nutrition digestion tolerance (real food like bananas, stroopwafels, electrolyte drink mixes).
