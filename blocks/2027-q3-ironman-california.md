# Master Weekly Hybrid Schedule: IRONMAN California Block

A high-volume, durability-focused weekly schedule built for **IRONMAN California (October 18, 2027)** in Sacramento. It prepares the body for a **2.4-mile downriver current swim**, **112 miles of continuous non-coasting aero cycling through the windy Sacramento Delta**, a **26.2-mile flat marathon finishing at the State Capitol**, and **stacked calisthenics** to safeguard the neck, spine, and posture against long-distance fatigue.

---

## 🎯 Block Overview & Objectives

* **Target Event:** IRONMAN California (Sacramento, CA)
* **Race Date:** Monday, October 18, 2027
* **Disciplines & Distances:**
  * 🏊 **Swim:** 2.4 miles (3.8 km) — Point-to-point downriver swim starting in the American River and finishing in the Sacramento River. Fast, current-assisted, wetsuit legal.
  * 🚴 **Bike:** 112 miles (180 km) — Two loops through the Sacramento Delta / farmland. Extremely flat with virtually zero coasting; requires holding aero tuck for 4.5–5.5+ hours against strong delta crosswinds and headwinds.
  * 🏃 **Run:** 26.2 miles (42.2 km) — Flat, 2-loop marathon along the Sacramento river trail, Old Sacramento, and Capitol Mall, finishing in front of the California State Capitol.
* **Target Time Benchmark:** Sub-10:00:00 (Swim ~54:00 [current-assisted], T1 ~4:30, Bike ~5:12:00 [21.5 mph @ 68–72% FTP], T2 ~3:00, Run ~3:35:00 [8:12/mi marathon pace] $\to$ Total: 9:48:30 with 11.5-minute buffer).

## 🏊🚴🏃 Detailed Workout Breakdown

### **Monday: Rest & Posture Decompression**
* **Cardio:** Complete Rest.
* **Mobility & Spine Protocol (20 mins):**
  1. **Dead Hangs from Bar:** 4 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds *(critical spinal decompression after heavy bike volume)*.
  2. **Prone Cobra / Y-T-W Holds:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps *(strengthens deep cervical neck extensors and lower trapezius)*.
  3. **Couch Stretch:** 2 minutes per side *(opens chronically shortened hip flexors from aero position)*.
  4. **Foam Roll Quads, IT Bands & Thoracic Spine:** 5 minutes deliberate rolling.

---

### **Tuesday: Big-Volume Swim + Marathon Tempo Run + Leg Strength**
* **Morning Swim: Race-Distance Continuous Endurance *(3,800m – 4,200m Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 400m easy swim + 200m pull buoy.
  * **Main Set (Long Continuous Aerobic Rhythm):**
    * $1 \times 1,500\text{m}$ continuous Zone 2 steady (Goal: 1:35 – 1:38 / 100m pace).
    * 1-minute rest.
    * $3 \times 500\text{m}$ pull buoy & paddles with 20s rest (building upper body catch).
    * $5 \times 100\text{m}$ fast aerobic on 1:40 turnaround.
  * **Cool-down:** 200m easy backstroke.
* **Afternoon Run: Marathon Tempo Run *(1:15 Total / 9–10 Miles)***
  * **Warm-up:** 15 mins easy Zone 2 + dynamic mobility.
  * **Main Set:** $2 \times 4\text{ miles}$ @ **Marathon Goal Pace (7:45 – 7:55 / mi)** with 3-minute easy jog recovery between sets.
  * **Cool-down:** 10 mins easy jog.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps/leg *(glute/quad stability)*.
  2. **Single-Leg Romanian Deadlifts:** 3 sets $\times 10$ reps/leg *(posterior chain control)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Glute Bridges:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg *(hip extension power)*.

---

### **Wednesday: Mid-Week Aero Endurance Ride + Upper Body A**
* **Primary Cardio: Sustained Aero Delta Simulation *(2:00 – 2:30 Total / 45–55 Miles)***
  * **Pacing:** Strict Zone 2 ($68\text{--}74\%$ FTP).
  * **Discipline:** Maintain full aerodynamic tuck for 25-minute continuous blocks without sitting up. Simulate the pancake-flat Sacramento Delta roads where pedaling is continuous with zero descent recovery.
  * **Fueling:** Practice $75\text{--}85\text{g}$ carbs/hr and $600\text{--}750\text{ mL}$ fluid/hr.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Ride):**
  1. **Strict Pull-ups:** 4 sets $\times 8\text{--}12$ reps *(lat strength for swim propulsion & posture)*.
  2. **Parallel Bar Dips or Ring Push-ups:** 4 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps.
  3. **Inverted Ring Rows:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{--}15$ reps *(horizontal scapular retraction to combat bike slouch)*.
  4. **Hanging Leg Raises / L-Sits:** 3 sets $\times 12\text{ reps}$ or 30s holds.

---

### **Thursday: CSS Swim + Aerobic Flush Run + Durability**
* **Morning Swim: Threshold / Critical Swim Speed (CSS) *(3,000m Total)***
  * **Warm-up:** 400m swim, 200m kick, $4 \times 50\text{m}$ build.
  * **Main Set:** $10 \times 200\text{m}$ @ **CSS Pace (1:30 – 1:34 / 100m)** with 15 seconds rest.
  * **Cool-down:** 200m easy choice.
* **Afternoon Run: Easy Aerobic Flush *(0:50 Total / 6 Miles)***
  * **Main Set:** Continuous easy running on grass or soft trail in low Zone 2 ($< 74\%$ HRmax).
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Immediately Post-Run):**
  1. **Nordic Hamstring Curls (or slow glute bridges):** 3 sets $\times 6\text{--}8$ reps *(eccentric hamstring resilience)*.
  2. **Tibialis Wall Raises:** 3 sets $\times 20$ reps *(anterior shin splint defense)*.
  3. **Single-Leg Eccentric Calf Raises:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps/leg (slow 4s drop).

---

### **Friday: Active Recovery Spin**
* **Primary Cardio: Flush Spin *(0:45 Total)***
  * **Main Set:** Low resistance, high cadence (90–95 RPM), Zone 1 ($< 55\%$ FTP).
* **Calisthenics:** None. Pre-saturate glycogen stores and electrolyte balance for weekend block.

---

### **Saturday: Overdistance Aero Long Ride + Brick Run**
* **Primary Cardio 1: Delta Simulation Long Ride *(4:30 – 5:45 Total / 85–112 Miles)***
  * **Target Power:** Controlled Zone 2 ($68\text{--}72\%$ FTP). Average heart rate capped at $< 75\%$ HRmax.
  * **Delta Wind Training:** Ride into prevailing headwinds or crosswinds staying calm and low in aero bars. Never fight the wind with surges; stay locked at target watts.
  * **Gut Training Target:** Consume **80–95g carbohydrates/hour** (liquid carb mix + gels) and **700–900 mg sodium/hour**. The gut is the fourth discipline of an Ironman.
* **Primary Cardio 2: Transition Brick Run *(0:25 – 0:30 Total / 3–4 Miles)***
  * **Execution:** Quick change directly into running shoes.
  * **Pacing:** Lock immediately into **Ironman Goal Marathon Pace (7:50 – 8:10 / mi)**. Focus on tall posture, short quick cadence (175–180 spm), and relaxed hands.

---

### **Sunday: Marathon Long Run + Upper Body B**
* **Primary Cardio: Progressive Aerobic Long Run *(2:00 – 2:30 Total / 15–18 Miles)***
  * **Miles 1–12:** Steady aerobic Zone 2 (8:15 – 8:40 / mi).
  * **Miles 13–16:** Settle into goal marathon pace (7:50 – 8:05 / mi) on pre-fatigued legs.
  * **Cool-down:** 0.5–1 mile easy walk/jog.
* **Stacked Calisthenics (Post-Run or PM):**
  1. **Archer Push-ups or Ring Push-ups:** 3 sets $\times 10\text{--}12$ reps.
  2. **Pull-ups:** 3 sets $\times$ clean reps.
  3. **Prone Neck Retractions & Face Pulls:** 3 sets $\times 15$ reps *(rehabilitate neck extensors)*.
  4. **Ab Wheel Rollouts / Hollow Body Hold:** 3 sets $\times 45\text{--}60$ seconds.

## 🎯 Course Specifics & Race Day Execution: IRONMAN California

* **Downriver Swim:** The American/Sacramento river current provides significant assistance (swimmers often record 5–10 minute PRs). Stay centered in the main current channel away from the shallow riverbanks until approaching the Tower Bridge exit.
* **Non-Coasting Delta Bike Course:** Unlike hilly courses where descents offer 15–20% coasting time, Sacramento has virtually **0% coasting**. Constant pedaling requires lower target power (68–72% FTP vs 78–82% in a 70.3) to prevent late-stage quad seizure.
* **Combating "Shermer's Neck":** Extreme aero duration can fatigue neck extensors. Perform regular cervical mobility drills on the bike every 30 minutes (look down at wheel for 5s, gently roll neck).
* **Marathon Discipline:** With thousands of cheering spectators at Capitol Mall and Old Sacramento, the initial 10K is dangerously easy to run too fast. Strictly enforce a ceiling of 8:00/mi for the first 10 miles.
