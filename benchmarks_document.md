## 🏋️ Lower-Body Strength & Durability Baselines

> High lower-leg durability protects against plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendinopathy, and shin splints.

| Exercise | Target Baseline | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nordic Curl** | 5 slow eccentric reps (5-sec descent) | Hamstring strain prevention |
| **Single-Leg Calf Raise** | 20 bodyweight reps off step | Achilles stiffness & calf endurance |
| **Tibialis Raise** | 25 continuous reps | Shin splint prevention |
| **Soleus Raise** | 20 reps @ 0.5x bodyweight | High-impact ground reaction force absorption |
| **Copenhagen Plank** | 45-sec hold / side | Adductor strength & pelvic control |
