# Endurance Strength & Mobility Library

A comprehensive reference for strength, mobility, and plyometric exercises used across all training blocks. 

---

## ⚡ Advanced Plyometrics

> **Rule of Engagement:** Always perform when fully fresh (post-activation, pre-run). High central nervous system (CNS) demand; quality over quantity.

### 1. Depth Jumps
* **Target:** Stretch-shortening cycle, tendon stiffness, landing mechanics.
* **Setup:** Stand on a box or step (12–18 inches high).
* **Execution:** Step off (do not hop) with one foot. Land on both feet simultaneously and immediately explode upward as high as possible. Minimize ground contact time.
* **Cue:** *"Treat the ground like a hot stove."*
* **Progression:** Start with 12" step $\rightarrow$ move to 18" step $\rightarrow$ single-leg landing landing/rebound.

### 2. Alternate Leg Bounding
* **Target:** Horizontal power output, hip extension power, running stride efficiency.
* **Execution:** Exaggerate your running stride for distance, pushing forcefully off the ground with each step. Drive the opposite knee high and extend the trailing leg fully.
* **Cue:** *"Maximize air time, bounce forward rather than up."*
* **Progression:** 3 sets of 30 meters $\rightarrow$ 3 sets of 50 meters.

### 3. Single-Leg Line Hops
* **Target:** Ankle reactivity, lateral stability, Achilles stiffness.
* **Setup:** Stand on one leg next to a line on the ground.
* **Execution:** Hop back and forth across the line laterally as fast as possible for 15–20 seconds.
* **Cue:** *"Stiff ankle, absorb and release energy immediately."*
* **Progression:** Side-to-side hops $\rightarrow$ forward-and-back hops.

### 4. Broad Jumps
* **Target:** Triple extension (hip, knee, ankle) and horizontal deceleration.
* **Execution:** Stand with feet hip-width apart. Load your hips back, swing arms backward, then explode forward landing softly on both feet in a athletic squat position.
* **Cue:** *"Explode forward, stick the landing like a gymnast."*

## 🦵 Lower-Body Strength

### Primary Mandatory Drivers

#### Bulgarian Split Squat
* **Target:** Quad strength, glute max drive, single-leg stance stability.
* **Setup:** Place back foot on a bench or chair, front foot 2–3 feet forward.
* **Execution:** Lower your back knee toward the floor until front thigh is parallel. Drive through the midfoot/heel of the front leg to stand.
* **Cue:** *"Keep torso slightly hinged forward to load the front glute."*

#### Nordic Curl
* **Target:** Eccentric hamstring strength, ACL & hamstring strain prevention.
* **Setup:** Anchor ankles under a heavy couch/barbell or have a partner hold them. Kneel tall on a padded surface.
* **Execution:** Lower your body forward as slowly as possible, keeping hips extended (no bending at the waist). Catch yourself in a push-up position when you break form.
* **Cue:** *"Fight gravity the entire way down."*

#### Single-Leg Calf Raise
* **Target:** Soleus strength, gastrocnemius power, Achilles tendon elasticity.
* **Setup:** Stand on the edge of a step on one foot, holding a wall for balance.
* **Execution:** Lower your heel below the step for a 2-second stretch, then drive up onto your big toe, holding the top position for 1 second.
* **Cue:** *"3 seconds down, 1 second hold at top."*

#### Tibialis Raise
* **Target:** Tibialis anterior strength, shin splint prevention, ankle dorsiflexion resilience.
* **Setup:** Lean upper back against a wall with heels ~18 inches away.
* **Execution:** Flex toes upward toward shins as high as possible. Pause at the top for 1 second before lowering slowly.
* **Cue:** *"Pull your toes to your kneecaps."*

---

### Supplementary Options

#### Step-Ups
* **Target:** Single-leg hip extension, knee tracking.
* **Execution:** Step onto a box/bench (knee at ~90 degrees). Drive through the lead heel to lift your body without pushing off the trailing toe.

#### Single-Leg RDL (Romanian Deadlift)
* **Target:** Hamstring loading, glute stability, balance.
* **Execution:** Hinge at the hips on one leg, extending trailing leg back in line with torso. Keep hips square to the floor.

#### Single-Leg Hip Thrust
* **Target:** Glute isolation, terminal hip extension lock-out.
* **Execution:** Upper back rested on a bench. Drive hips to the ceiling on one leg, squeezing glute at the top.

#### Pogo Hops
* **Target:** Reactive ankle stiffness, lower-leg elastic recoil.
* **Execution:** Hop repeatedly on the balls of your feet with stiff knees, using only ankle extension to propel upward.

## 🏋️ Upper Body & Core

### Primary Mandatory Drivers

#### Copenhagen Plank
* **Target:** Adductor strength, pelvic alignment, groin strain prevention.
* **Setup:** Side plank with top leg rested on a bench/chair and bottom leg hanging or tucked.
* **Execution:** Hold a rigid side plank position.
* **Cue:** *"Squeeze the bench with your top leg to keep hips high."*

#### Side Plank + Top-Leg Lift
* **Target:** Gluteus medius, lateral trunk stability.
* **Execution:** In a side plank, raise top leg 12 inches up while maintaining a straight line from head to heels.

#### Hollow Body Hold / Ab Wheel Rollout
* **Target:** Anti-extension core strength, posterior pelvic tilt control.
* **Execution (Hollow Body):** Press lower back flat into the floor, extend arms overhead and legs out low to the floor.

---

### Supplementary Options

#### Pull-ups / Chin-ups
* **Target:** Lats, upper back, grip strength for postural stability.

#### Inverted Rows / Dips
* **Target:** Horizontal pulling and pushing mechanics to balance running posture.

#### Dead Bug / Pallof Press
* **Target:** Anti-rotation and anti-extension trunk stability.
